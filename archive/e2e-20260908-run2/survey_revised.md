# World Models for Games: A Survey

## Abstract
This survey covers 5 themes over 15 citation-ready studies, spanning Procedural Content Generation Mechanisms to Interactive and Controllable Generation Frameworks. Every claim carries the tag of the paper whose evidence supports it. GameFactory is a framework for action-controlled and scene-generalizable game video generation, using generative videos to autonomously create new game content. [paper:10.48550/arxiv.2501.08325]. PCGRL: Procedural Content Generation via Reinforcement Learning investigates how reinforcement learning can be used to train agents that construct game levels and other types of game content. [paper:10.1609/aiide.v16i1.7416]. Leveraging Procedural Generation to Benchmark Reinforcement Learning introduces Procgen Benchmark, a suite of 16 procedurally generated game-like environments in which agents must learn robust policies and generalize across ever-changing levels. [paper:10.48550/arxiv.1912.01588].

## Introduction
Game intelligence increasingly rests on models that learn how a world evolves rather than on hand-written rules alone; whether such models can support planning, generation, and fair evaluation is the question this survey addresses. Each theme that follows gathers the evidence for one capability and states where that evidence stops.

Procedural Content Generation Mechanisms surveys covers methods and algorithms used to automatically generate game content such as levels, items, or narratives within world models. Benchmark Datasets and Evaluation Metrics collects the work on encompasses the standardized datasets used for training and testing world models, along with the quantitative and qualitative metrics employed to assess their performance. Internal Simulation and Planning Capabilities follows the evidence for focuses on the model's ability to simulate future states, reason about actions, and plan sequences within the learned environment. Latent Architectures for World Representation maps what is known about describes the underlying neural network structures and latent space designs used to encode and represent the game world dynamics. On Interactive and Controllable Generation Frameworks, the surveyed work converges on addresses frameworks that allow user interaction, steering, or control over the generated world content and behaviors.
